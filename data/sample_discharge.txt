Patient diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Hypertension.

Discharge Medications:
- Metformin 500 mg twice daily after meals.
- Amlodipine 5 mg once daily.
- Atorvastatin 20 mg at bedtime.

Diet:
Follow a diabetic-friendly, low-salt diet. Avoid sugary beverages, processed foods, and excessive carbohydrates.

Activity:
Resume light daily activities. Walk for at least 30 minutes daily as tolerated. Avoid strenuous exercise for one week.

Monitoring:
Check blood glucose twice daily and blood pressure once daily. Maintain a record of readings.

Follow-up:
Visit your primary care physician within 7 days with your blood glucose and blood pressure records.

Warning Signs:
Seek immediate medical attention if you experience chest pain, severe shortness of breath, confusion, persistent vomiting, loss of consciousness, or blood glucose below 70 mg/dL with symptoms.
